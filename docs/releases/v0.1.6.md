# PGRazor v0.1.6

## New in the Quick Menu

- **Switch account:** Manage Google and PTC accounts without opening the full settings menu. Each account keeps its own settings, last location, cooldown history, favorites and saved routes. Switching stops automation and restarts the game using the selected account's saved session.
- **In-game Routes:** Find nearby Routes, walk to their start, follow their recorded path, and wait for reward confirmation before moving to the next Route. Choose a completion limit and tap the draggable Routes icon to view progress.

[View the Quick Menu with new features marked](https://github.com/jpitty03/PGRazor/releases/download/v0.1.6/release-highlight.png)

## Other changes

- Delete inactive saved accounts and their local settings and sessions. This does not delete the Pokémon GO account.
- Automatically dismiss Adventure Sync prompts when location spoofing is enabled and Android location permission is denied.
- Show the PGRazor version beside the theme button in Settings.

## Account setup

Sign in once per account to save its session. Sessions are encrypted on-device, excluded from backups and settings exports, and do not include saved passwords. Expired or revoked sessions may require sign-in again. Existing settings become the first profile; new profiles start with defaults. LivePokeMap login remains shared, with separate filters per account.

Account management and in-game Routes are experimental. Google account switching was verified in both directions on Android, including restoration of each account's location and settings. Automated checks cover profile isolation, session transactions and recovery; PTC switching and expired-session fallback still need live testing. In-game Routes were tested on MuMu and Android.

## Installation

Download **PGRazor.apk** and install it over your existing PGRazor installation to preserve settings. The same APK supports ARM64 Android phones and MuMu.
